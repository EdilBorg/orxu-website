# ORXU Website — Project Brief

**Status:** Specification phase  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-12  
**Repository:** https://github.com/EdilBorg/orxu-website

This document is the primary source of truth for brand identity, positioning, objectives, and communication rules. All other specification documents must remain consistent with this brief.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**ORXU** is an independent software product portfolio brand.

It provides institutional identity and coherence for a set of software products while allowing each product to maintain its own independent identity, visual language, website, and commercial strategy.

ORXU is **not**:
- A software development agency
- A consulting firm
- A services company that builds software for third parties

ORXU is a **portfolio brand** that owns and presents its own software products.

### Legal Status

The legal registration status of ORXU has not been established.  
**Do not claim that ORXU is a legally registered company, corporation, or limited liability entity in any jurisdiction.**

---

## 2. Positioning

ORXU positions itself as a credible, professional, international software brand focused on useful, well-designed products.

The institutional website must:

- Establish a clear online presence for the brand
- Present the product portfolio with editorial quality and clarity
- Explain the purpose and audience of each product
- Direct visitors to the respective product websites when available
- Provide institutional contact information
- Support international visitors through a bilingual structure (English primary, Portuguese secondary)
- Communicate credibility without fabricated claims

The website must never overstate the maturity, market presence, or commercial achievements of the brand or its products.

---

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- Businesses and organizations looking for specialized software tools
- Software users and operators
- Entrepreneurs and professionals evaluating or adopting new tools

The brand maintains a global communication strategy. However, the website must not claim commercial operations, offices, customers, or presence in specific countries unless those facts are later confirmed.

---

## 4. Objectives of the Institutional Website

1. **Institutional presence** — Create a professional, trustworthy online home for the ORXU brand.
2. **Portfolio clarity** — Present each product with its own purpose, audience, and status.
3. **Traffic direction** — Guide interested visitors to the individual product websites or contact points.
4. **Contact channel** — Provide a clear, low-friction way for legitimate inquiries.
5. **Bilingual accessibility** — Serve English and Portuguese speakers through dedicated routes.
6. **Long-term maintainability** — Keep the site simple enough that content and structure can evolve without technical debt.

The website is **not** a marketing funnel designed to convert at all costs, nor a SaaS application, nor a content marketing platform.

## 6. Communication Principles

### Required tone
- Professional, clear, and restrained
- Editorial rather than promotional
- Confident without exaggeration
- Transparent about development status

### Factual accuracy rules (non-negotiable)

- Never invent clients, revenue figures, partnerships, certifications, awards, team size, or commercial achievements.
- Never claim legal registration or specific company status that has not been confirmed.
- Never claim that a product is “launched”, “available worldwide”, or “fully featured” unless that is accurate at the time of writing.
- Distinguish clearly between current capabilities and planned capabilities.
- Do not invent contact channels (phone numbers, WhatsApp, physical addresses, social profiles) that have not been confirmed.
- Do not invent domain names or claim ownership of domains that have not been confirmed.

### Known institutional contact
- Email: `orxugroup@gmail.com`
- No confirmed WhatsApp number at this time.

---

## 7. Restrictions and Anti-Patterns

The following are explicitly forbidden:

- Presenting ORXU as a software agency or service provider that builds products for others
- Using generic SaaS template language (“The all-in-one platform…”, “Trusted by thousands…”, “Industry-leading…”)
- Forcing the three products to share the same visual identity or color system as the institutional brand
- Creating false urgency, fake scarcity, or invented social proof
- Adding features or pages that turn the institutional site into an application (accounts, dashboards, payments, etc.)
- Introducing unnecessary technical complexity that conflicts with the static-site philosophy
