# ORXU Website — Content Specification

**Status:** Specification phase  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-12  
**Depends on:** `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md`

This document defines the content structure, messaging approach, and language strategy for every major page type. It ensures factual accuracy and consistency across English and Portuguese versions.

---

## 1. General Content Principles

- English is the primary language; Portuguese is the secondary language.
- Every content page must exist in both languages (or be clearly planned).
- Never mix languages on the same page.
- Tone: professional, clear, restrained, editorial.
- Distinguish **current capabilities** from **planned capabilities**.
- Never invent clients, metrics, partnerships, certifications, awards, team members, or commercial achievements.
- Never claim legal registration or specific company status that has not been confirmed.
- Known institutional contact: `orxugroup@gmail.com`  
  No confirmed WhatsApp number or physical address at this time.

---

## 2. Homepage Content Structure

The homepage is the primary institutional statement and portfolio introduction. It must not reduce the three products to three identical generic cards.

### Recommended structure (both languages)

1. **Hero / Opening statement**
   - Short, confident institutional positioning of ORXU as a software product portfolio brand.
   - Avoid generic slogans.

2. **Portfolio introduction**
   - Brief explanation that ORXU is the institutional home for a set of independent software products.

3. **Editorial product presentations**
   - Each product receives its own section with differentiated visual and textual treatment.
   - For each product include:
     - Product name
     - One-sentence purpose
     - Short paragraph expanding purpose and audience
     - Development status (transparent)
     - Link to the individual product page on this site
     - External product website link when available

4. **Closing institutional note or contact invitation**
   - Calm invitation to learn more or get in touch.

### Product presentation order (suggested)

1. Korvex
2. Maps Leads Finder
3. BOWIS

Order can be adjusted later based on priority, but the editorial approach must remain non-uniform.

---

## 3. About Page Content Structure

### Purpose
Explain what ORXU is and what it is not, in a factual and restrained manner.

### Recommended structure

1. **Opening definition**
   - ORXU is an independent software product portfolio brand.

2. **Portfolio model**
   - Each product maintains its own identity, visual language, website, and commercial strategy.
   - ORXU provides institutional coherence without absorbing the products.

3. **What ORXU is not**
   - Not a software development agency.
   - Not a services company that builds software for third parties.

4. **Approach**
   - Focus on useful, well-designed products.
   - Transparent communication about development status.

5. **Contact**
   - Institutional email only: `orxugroup@gmail.com`

Do not invent team biographies, office locations, founding stories with unverified details, or mission statements that overclaim.

---

## 4. Product Page Content Structure

Each product page (English + Portuguese) follows the same logical structure while allowing editorial variation in length and emphasis.

### Shared structure

1. **Product name + short positioning line**
2. **Purpose**
   - Clear statement of what the product is for.
3. **Audience**
   - Who the product is intended to serve.
4. **Key directions / capabilities**
   - Present current and planned capabilities separately and clearly.
   - Use language such as “currently focused on…”, “in development…”, “planned direction includes…” when appropriate.
5. **Development status**
   - Explicit and honest (e.g. “In development”, “Early development”).
6. **External link**
   - When a product website exists, provide a clear, prominent link.
   - When it does not exist (BOWIS), state that a product website is not yet available.

### Product-specific guidance

#### Korvex
- Focus: WhatsApp automation, visual workflows, conversation management, AI-assisted functionality, token-based usage.
- External URL: https://korvex-8.polsia.io
- Status: In development — do not present all planned capabilities as already available.

#### Maps Leads Finder
- Focus: Business prospecting and lead research by category and location, information organization, filtering, multiple data sources, duplicate management.
- External URL: https://maps-leads-finder-saas.onrender.com
- Status: In development — do not claim full commercial launch or invent coverage guarantees.

#### BOWIS
- Focus: Point-of-sale and commercial management — sales, product and customer management, operations, records and reporting.
- External URL: Not confirmed — do not invent one.
- Status: Early development.

## 5. Contact Page Content Structure

### Required elements

- Clear heading
- Short introductory text explaining the purpose of the contact channel (institutional inquiries)
- Primary contact method: email `orxugroup@gmail.com`
- Optional simple note that responses are handled as capacity allows

### Forbidden

- Invented phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, physical addresses, or social media accounts
- Contact forms that require a backend (unless a pure static form solution is later approved and remains zero-backend)
- Fake “offices” or “global presence” claims

A simple `mailto:` link is sufficient and preferred at this stage.

---

## 6. Legal Pages

### Privacy Policy
- Must exist in both languages.
- Cover basic data practices relevant to a static institutional website (cookies if any, analytics if introduced later, contact form data if any, etc.).
- Be honest about the current limited data collection footprint.
- Do not copy generic policies from other companies without adaptation.

### Terms of Use
- Must exist in both languages.
- Cover website usage, intellectual property (ORXU and product names), limitations of liability, and governing considerations at a high level.
- Avoid inventing complex legal structures.

Exact legal text should be reviewed carefully before publication. Placeholder or draft text may be used during development and replaced with final versions.

---

## 7. English and Portuguese Content Strategy

### Translation approach
- Portuguese versions must be proper translations, not machine-translated afterthoughts.
- Maintain the same factual claims and structure.
- Adapt phrasing for natural Portuguese while preserving meaning and tone.
- Product names remain the same across languages (Korvex, Maps Leads Finder, BOWIS).

### Consistency rules
- Status statements must match exactly in meaning between languages.
- External product URLs remain identical.
- Contact email remains identical.
- Development status language must stay synchronized when content is updated.

---

## 8. Distinction Between Existing and Planned Capabilities

This distinction is mandatory on product pages and should be reflected on the homepage where capabilities are mentioned.

**Recommended phrasing patterns:**

- Current: “Currently focused on…”, “Supports…”, “Provides…”
- Planned / In development: “Planned direction includes…”, “In development…”, “Future capabilities under consideration…”

Never present a planned capability as if it is already shipping.

---

## 9. Metadata and SEO Content

For every page:

- Unique, descriptive `<title>` (include “ORXU” where natural)
- Unique meta description (accurate, not keyword-stuffed)
- Correct Open Graph title and description when social cards are implemented

Titles and descriptions must be written in the language of the page.
