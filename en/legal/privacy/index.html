Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the ORXU institutional website. It describes, in general terms, how information is handled on this static site.

Information we collect

This website is a static institutional site. It does not require accounts and does not operate a backend application. In the ordinary course of browsing:

  • Standard server or hosting logs may record technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This depends on the hosting provider (for example Cloudflare Pages).
  • If you contact us by email, the content of your message and your email address will be received and stored as part of normal correspondence.

Use of information

Email correspondence is used only to respond to inquiries and manage legitimate institutional communication. Log data, where collected by the host, is used for security, performance and basic diagnostics.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently implement third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

Third parties

The site may be served through Cloudflare or similar static hosting infrastructure. Product websites linked from this site (Korvex, Maps Leads Finder, etc.) are separate and may have their own practices.

Contact

For privacy-related questions about this institutional website, contact: orxugroup@gmail.com.

This document is provided for general information. It is not legal advice. The practices described may be refined as the site and its infrastructure evolve.
