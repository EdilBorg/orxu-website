Product

Korvex

WhatsApp automation software with a focus on visual workflows and conversation management.

In development

Purpose

Korvex is directed at teams and operators who need structured automation around WhatsApp conversations. The product direction includes visual automation workflows, conversation management, WhatsApp integration, AI-assisted functionality and a token-based usage model.

Audience

Businesses and professionals who manage customer or operational conversations on WhatsApp and want clearer automation and organisation.

Current status

Korvex is in development. Not every planned capability is available yet. The product continues to evolve; claims about features should be understood in that context.
