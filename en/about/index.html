About

ORXU is a software product portfolio brand.

It provides institutional identity for a set of independent software products, each with its own direction and commercial approach.

What ORXU is

ORXU is an independent software product portfolio brand. It serves as the institutional home for specialized software products developed and maintained under the ORXU umbrella.

Each product in the portfolio maintains its own identity, visual language, website and commercial strategy. ORXU provides coherence at the institutional level without absorbing the individual products.

What ORXU is not

ORXU is not a software development agency, a consulting firm, or a services company that builds software for third parties. It does not present itself as a team for hire.

Approach

The focus is on useful, well-designed products and transparent communication about their development status. Claims about maturity, availability or commercial reach are kept factual and restrained.

Contact

For institutional inquiries related to the portfolio or its products, use the email address listed on the contact page.
